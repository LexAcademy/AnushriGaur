Payment & Enrolment Process

  1. Select Your Programme: Identify the programme you wish to enrol in, such as UPSC Law Optional Test Series, Personalised Mentorship, DAF Analysis or Mock Interview.
  2. Check the Programme Fee: Refer to the applicable programme fee before making payment.
  3. Make the Payment: Transfer the applicable fee using the bank account or UPI details provided above.
  4. Save Payment Proof: Take a screenshot of the successful transaction and note the transaction reference number.
  5. Share Details on WhatsApp: Send the payment screenshot along with your full name, mobile number, selected programme, amount and transaction reference number to Lex Academy.
  6. Enrolment Verification: The academy will verify the payment and confirm your enrolment through WhatsApp.
  7. Programme Access: After confirmation, the academy will share the relevant programme details, instructions and access information.
Important: Enrolment will be considered confirmed only after the academy verifies the payment and communicates confirmation to the student. Please do not treat a payment screenshot alone as confirmation of enrolment.
