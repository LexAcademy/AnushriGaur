Eligibility

Who can apply for Gujarat Civil Judge?

The following reflects the eligibility framework under the latest detailed Gujarat High Court Civil Judge recruitment advertisement. Always re-check the new 2026 detailed advertisement before applying.

Law Degree

A candidate must possess a Degree in Law from a university recognised by law in India.

Legal Practice

The detailed recruitment advertisement required candidates under the advocate route to be practising in courts of civil and/or criminal jurisdiction on the last date of application.

AIBE Requirement

Candidates who obtained their Law degree from academic year 2009–10 onwards were required under the latest detailed advertisement to have passed the All India Bar Examination for recognition as a practising advocate, subject to the specific conditions stated in that recruitment.

General Age Limit

The latest detailed advertisement prescribed an upper age limit of 35 years for General Category candidates on the last date of application.

Reserved Categories

The corresponding upper age limit prescribed for SC, ST, SEBC, EWS and eligible PwBD candidates was 38 years.

Computer Knowledge

Candidates were also required to possess certification evidencing basic knowledge of computer application or operation.

Age and eligibility caution: these conditions are drawn from the latest detailed recruitment advertisement. They should not be treated as confirmation that every condition will remain identical in the detailed 2026 recruitment notice.

Gujarat-Specific Preparation

Do not prepare Gujarat Judiciary exactly like an all-India law exam

Certain Gujarat-specific legislation appears expressly in the Civil Judge syllabus and deserves separate revision.

Gujarat Prohibition Act, 1949 Expressly included in the criminal-law syllabus.
Gujarat Prevention of Gambling Act, 1887 A state-specific criminal law included in the syllabus.
Gujarat Court Fees Act, 2004 Expressly included in the civil-law syllabus.
Gujarati Language The language-proficiency requirement can be independently qualifying for candidates who do not meet the SSC/HSC Gujarati exemption.
New Criminal Codes

BNS, BNSS and BSA are part of the preparation framework

The latest detailed Gujarat Civil Judge syllabus expressly referred to the new criminal codes alongside the older enactments.

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

Prepare the substantive criminal-law concepts while becoming familiar with the changed statutory structure and section numbering.

Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023

Procedural concepts such as arrest, investigation, bail, charge, trial and criminal-court procedure remain critical.

Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023

Revise relevancy, admissibility, presumptions, documentary and electronic evidence while mapping earlier Evidence Act concepts to the new statute.

Preparation Strategy

A practical way to structure your Gujarat Judiciary preparation

The objective is not simply to finish the syllabus. Your preparation must support both prelims recall and descriptive Mains writing.

Build the core procedural foundation first

Give substantial attention to CPC, criminal procedure and evidence. These subjects demand both statutory accuracy and application.

Study substantive criminal law with the new codes

Do not rely only on old IPC/CrPC/Evidence notes. Maintain a mapping between important old provisions and BNS, BNSS and BSA.

Strengthen the major civil-law cluster

Contract, Specific Relief, Limitation, Transfer of Property, Constitution, Partnership and allied civil laws need systematic revision.

Create a separate Gujarat-law revision file

State-specific statutes should not remain scattered through your notes. Keep their important definitions, offences, powers and procedural provisions together for rapid revision.

Practise MCQs with negative marking in mind

The latest detailed prelims pattern penalises wrong or multiple answers. Practise accuracy, elimination techniques and controlled attempts rather than maximising attempts blindly.

Begin descriptive writing before prelims is over

Mains preparation requires legal organisation, provision-based answers, application and concise conclusions. Waiting until the prelims result to begin descriptive writing can compress your preparation window.

Prepare Gujarati independently if applicable

If you need the Gujarati Language Test, treat it as a separate qualifying paper with regular essay, grammar, translation and comprehension practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Gujarat Judiciary Civil Judge FAQ

How many Gujarat Civil Judge vacancies are expected in 2026?

The Gujarat High Court issued Notification RC/0719/2026 dated 24 April 2026 notifying approximately 237 regular existing and future Civil Judge vacancies. The number can be altered by the High Court.

Has the complete Gujarat Civil Judge 2026 advertisement been issued?

The vacancy notification states that the recruitment process will be initiated separately. Candidates should therefore check Gujarat High Court's Current Openings page for the detailed advertisement, application dates and final examination conditions.

What is the Gujarat Civil Judge prelims pattern?

Under the latest detailed recruitment advertisement, the Preliminary Examination consisted of a 100-mark MCQ paper of two hours with 0.33 negative marking for every wrong or multiple answer.

Is the Gujarat Judiciary prelims paper in Gujarati?

The latest detailed advertisement prescribed the Preliminary Examination question paper in English only.

Is Gujarati compulsory for Gujarat Judiciary?

Candidates who have passed SSC or HSC with Gujarati as a subject were exempt from the separate Gujarati proficiency test under the latest detailed advertisement. Other candidates were required to take the 50-mark Gujarati test and obtain the prescribed qualifying marks.

What is the Gujarat Civil Judge Mains pattern?

The latest detailed advertisement prescribed two descriptive papers: Paper I Criminal Law and Paper II Civil Law, each carrying 100 marks and having a duration of three hours.

Do prelims marks count in final Gujarat Judiciary merit?

Under the latest detailed advertisement, Preliminary Examination and Gujarati Language Test marks were not counted for final merit. The Select List was prepared on the basis of Main Written Examination and viva-voce marks.

How many marks are allotted to Gujarat Judiciary viva?

The latest detailed Civil Judge advertisement prescribed a 50-mark viva-voce with a minimum qualifying requirement of 40%.

Should I prepare BNS, BNSS and BSA for Gujarat Judiciary?

Yes. The latest detailed Gujarat Civil Judge syllabus expressly included the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam alongside the older criminal-law enactments.
