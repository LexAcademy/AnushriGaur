What UPSC Law Optional preparation requires

Law Optional demands more than reading statutes and judgments. Aspirants must identify the precise demand of the question, organise the answer logically, use relevant constitutional provisions, statutes and case law, apply legal principles to the issue and conclude within the available time.

An answer-writing-first approach

The preparation method at Lex Academy emphasises a repeatable cycle: attempt, review, identify gaps, revise and attempt again. This approach is intended to improve clarity, structure, legal application and time management.

Areas of support

Students can use Lex Academy for Law Optional test series, mid-length and full-length practice, mock simulation, customised tests and one-to-one mentorship depending on their preparation stage.
