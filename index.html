Experience that informs the method

Ms. Anushri Gaur is an electrical and electronics engineer. She later earned an LLB and subsequently completed an LLM in business law. Currently, she is having 5+ years of experience as practicing advocate in Jodhpur High Court and District Courts in Rajasthan. She brings together legal training, competitive-examination experience and a practical understanding of how law must be analysed and presented under time pressure. Her preparation journey includes two UPSC Civil Services Mains and interview appearances in 2022, with 283 marks in Law Optional Mains, 122 marks in ethics GS4 exam and secured Rank 387 in CLAT PG 2022.

She is also listed as a recommended candidate in the Rajasthan Administrative Service (RPSC RAS) Examination 2021 & 2024. She successfully qualified for the Gujarat Judicial Service Mains examination and appeared for the interview in 2025. These milestones reflect sustained engagement with demanding, law-oriented competitive examinations.

She has also secured a meritorious rank in the 10th National IT Aptitude Test and an ‘A’ grade in the Global ‘TOEL’ English-language test.

What this means for your preparation

The teaching approach is designed to help aspirants identify the precise demand of a question, organise the answer logically, support arguments with relevant constitutional provisions, statutes and case law, apply legal principles to the issue, and reach a concise conclusion—while managing time across the paper.

Rather than relying only on reading, students are encouraged to build a repeatable practice cycle: attempt, review, identify gaps, revise and attempt again. Mentorship can be used to clarify priorities and work on individual answer-writing difficulties.
