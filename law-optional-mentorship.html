UPSC Law Optional Mentorship

One-to-one mentorship is intended for aspirants who want focused help identifying gaps, structuring answers and building a practical improvement plan.

Enquire NowView Programmes

Diagnose recurring answer-writing gaps

Mentorship can be used to identify recurring weaknesses such as incomplete issue identification, weak structure, inadequate authority, poor application or time-management problems.

Decode the exact demand of a question

Students are guided to distinguish between descriptive, analytical and problem-based demands and to decide what the examiner is actually asking before writing.

Build a repeatable answer structure

A useful legal answer commonly moves from introduction and legal rule to authority, application and a concise conclusion. The exact structure should remain responsive to the question.

Turn feedback into the next practice step

The purpose of mentoring is not only to discuss one answer but to identify specific next steps for practice, revision and improved performance in subsequent attempts.
